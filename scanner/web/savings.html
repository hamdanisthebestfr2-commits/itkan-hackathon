Where the numbers come from

athenahealth doesn't publish prices. Third-party reviews estimate athenaOne at about 4-8% of monthly collections (sometimes over 10%), with small practices quoted at the high end. That's roughly $65K-$185K/yr for 3 providers and $150K-$450K/yr for 5-10 providers.

To be fair: the percentage includes billing services (people who work claims and denials). Iris replaces most of that work with AI that staff review, which is why it can charge a flat price. Estimates for illustration only.
